Plan

Speed through the water is your paddling or rowing speed in still water. Speed over the bottom adds the tidal stream along the course. Night is sunset to sunrise.

Stops

Breaks ashore, long rests and waits for the current go to the landing that costs the least extra paddling: a past stop (where teams in 2024 to 2026 stayed 15 minutes or more) or a mapped beach, campsite, launch or marina within a mile of the course. The trip in and out counts as paddling. Click a row to set the map time.

Boat and crew

Hull speed (1.34 × √waterline in ft): the plan never asks for more speed through the water than this.
